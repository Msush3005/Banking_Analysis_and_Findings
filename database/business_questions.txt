BANKING ANALYTICS PORTFOLIO — BUSINESS QUESTIONS

1. Which customer segments contribute the most total deposits and transaction value?
2. Which customers are high-value but have low product penetration?
3. Which customers have high income but low account balances or low engagement?
4. Which branches have the highest deposits, customer counts and transaction activity?
5. Which branches have high loan exposure but weaker credit quality?
6. Which loan types have the highest approval volume and loan exposure?
7. How does loan-to-income ratio vary across income bands and occupations?
8. Which approved loans have the highest estimated debt-service burden?
9. What share of approved loans are in High/Critical risk bands?
10. Are high-risk loans concentrated in particular cities, occupations or loan types?
11. Which customers show unusually high transaction values or high-value transaction activity?
12. How do transaction volumes and values change month by month?
13. Which channels (mobile, internet, ATM, branch) dominate transaction activity?
14. Which customers have inactive accounts but historically meaningful balances?
15. Which account types have the strongest balances and transaction activity?
16. Where are the strongest cross-sell opportunities: customers with accounts but no approved loan?
17. What customer characteristics are associated with higher loan approval or rejection rates?
18. Which branches should receive retention, cross-sell or risk-monitoring attention?
